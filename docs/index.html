Abstract

Feed-forward scene reconstruction has become a core component of autonomous driving. It provides an efficient and scalable way to reconstruct scenes, which supports major downstream tasks including simulation, world modeling, and closed-loop reinforcement learning. We present X-Splatter, a surround-view, feed-forward 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) framework. X-Splatter is built on a VGGT backbone and introduces three key components: a Depth Anchoring Fine-tuning (DAF) process that shifts geometry from relative space to metric space; a Cross-camera Fusion Module (CFM) that applies cross-camera attention throughout the network to enforce consistency across views; and a Static–Dynamic Decomposition Learning (SDDL) strategy that combines dynamic gating, static-promoting regularization, and intermediate-frame supervision to discourage decomposition shortcuts and support velocity learning, without bounding boxes, tracklets, or optical-flow supervision.

X-Splatter is trained and evaluated on all surrounding views, including the side views that share the least content with the front camera. It achieves state-of-the-art full-surround reconstruction on the Waymo Open Dataset, in both full-scene and dynamic-region novel-view synthesis, with the lowest metric-depth RMSE. The same model also generalizes zero-shot to nuScenes and Argoverse 2, achieving the best rendering PSNR and metric-depth accuracy on both datasets.

Method

DAFDepth Anchoring Fine-tuning

Adapts a pretrained VGGT-Ω backbone from relative to metric depth, using sparse LiDAR plus a frozen teacher for the pixels LiDAR misses. The geometry is then frozen, so motion learning cannot trade depth for motion.

CFMCross-camera Fusion Module

Lets each camera attend to all cameras at neighboring frames, so an object seen only partly in one view draws on the evidence another view provides.

SDDLStatic–Dynamic Decomposition

A per-Gaussian gate splits shared static content from velocity-warped dynamic content. Static-promoting regularization and intermediate-frame supervision stop the model from explaining the scene with motion it does not have, without boxes, tracks, or flow labels.

Held-out front-camera views. Waymo is in domain; nuScenes and Argoverse 2 are zero-shot, with no training on either dataset. Depth uses a shared 0–80 m colormap; white marks sky and pixels without LiDAR or a rendered value.

Cross-camera consistency

Where two cameras overlap, a consistent reconstruction should place the same surface at the same 3D position from both. Each panel unprojects the two cameras' own depth over their shared field of view, seen from above; LiDAR is in black. Over 202 Waymo scenes, X-Splatter places the same surface 0.72 m apart on average, against 2.51 m for STORM.
